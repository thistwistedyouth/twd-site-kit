# The 30 minute call: run sheet

Before the call: the form is in, you have read it, and you know what is missing.

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Welcome. Say what the call is for. **Ask for consent to record, say it will be turned into text and the audio deleted, and wait for a yes before pressing record.** |
| 3 to 5 | Read back what the form says in one breath, and ask what is wrong or missing. |
| 5 to 17 | Who they are and who they help (questions 1 to 9 in the questionnaire). Let silences happen. |
| 17 to 23 | How they work and practicalities (questions 10 to 12). |
| 23 to 27 | Voice and look and feel (13 and 14). Show one or two example sites if they have no view. |
| 27 to 30 | Close (15). Say what happens next: a sheet of everything you will put on the site, which they confirm before anything goes live. Agree the date for photos. Stop the recording. |

## During
- Do not type numbers, registration lines or addresses from speech. Ask them to put them in the form.
- If they mention a client or a case, move on and note to leave it out.
- Say "I will check that" for anything you are unsure of. Never guess.

## After
1. Transcribe to text (names of anyone treated removed).
2. Delete the audio.
3. Update the status table in the client `CLAUDE.md`.
