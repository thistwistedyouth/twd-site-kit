# Photo shot list

Send this to the client with the form link. Phone photos are fine if they follow the tips.

## What to send
1. **A clear portrait.** Head and shoulders, face lit from the front, plain background. Looking at the camera, relaxed.
2. **A second, more natural shot.** Working, or somewhere they like, or a smile away from the camera.
3. **The space.** Their room or office if clients visit. No clients, no notes or papers in view, no personal details visible.
4. **Logo.** The best copy they have (a png or svg, not a screenshot).
5. **Anything that says who they are.** A bookshelf, a walk by the sea, an object they use. Optional.

## Tips
- Natural daylight from a window. Face the window, not the sun.
- Phone held at eye height, in landscape for the space and portrait for the portrait.
- No filters. No sunglasses. Wipe the lens.
- Send the original file, not a screenshot or a message-app copy, which loses quality.

## Rules for you
- Only use photos the client confirms they own or can use. Note it in `02-photos/`.
- No photos of other people unless the client confirms permission.
- Give every photo a plain description (alt text) of what it shows.
- Delete any photo you do not use once the site is live.
