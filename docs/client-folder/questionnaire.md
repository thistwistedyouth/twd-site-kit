# Questions for the call

The form gives you the typed details. The call is for the story and the voice. Ask a few at a time, listen, and record with consent.

For each question: what to ask, and why.

## Who they are
1. "How did you come to do this work?" Gives the About page its human thread.
2. "What do you want people to know about you before they get in touch?" Their own priority.
3. "What training and experience would you want a new client to know about?" Qualifications in their own words. Anything with a number or a body name goes in the typed form or is checked on a document.

## Who they help
4. "Who are the people you most enjoy working with?" 
5. "What is happening in their lives when they first contact you?" Their words for the problem, not ours.
6. "What do they often worry about before they book?" Feeds the FAQ.

## How they work
7. "What does a first session look like?" 
8. "How do you work? What can someone expect over time?" 
9. "What is not a good fit?" Honest limits build trust.

## Practicalities
10. "Where, when, and how long? Online, in person, evenings?" 
11. "Fees and how people pay, if you want it on the site." Never guess. Leave out if unsure.
12. "How would you like people to contact you?" Check it matches the form.

## Voice
13. "How would you describe your style in three words?" 
14. "Is there anything you would never say to a client or never want on your site?" 

## Close
15. "Is there anything I have not asked that matters to you?"
