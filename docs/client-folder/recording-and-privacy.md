# Recording and privacy notes

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. If in doubt, ask.

## Consent wording (say this at the start of the call)
"I would like to record this call so I can write up your site accurately. I will turn it into text and then delete the recording. I will keep the text only until your site is live, and nobody else will see it. Are you happy for me to record?"

Wait for a clear yes. If no, take notes and say so. Note the consent in the status table.

## Keep it to business information
Ask only about the practice. If a practitioner mentions anyone they treat, do not record or keep it. Strip it from the text before anything else is done with it.

## Where it lives
- A private folder only you can open. Never this public repo.
- Never paste audio, a transcript or the form export into any tool you do not control the privacy of. When pasting into an AI, use an account whose settings you have checked and paste only what is needed.
- Do not put credentials in the client folder. Send logins separately.

## Deleting
| Item | Delete when |
|---|---|
| Audio recording | As soon as the transcript is made |
| Transcript | When the site is live and confirmed |
| Form submission and uploaded files on the form site | When the site is live |
| Photos you do not use | When the site is live |
| Client folder | Keep the status table and the final brief for as long as you support the site, then remove |

## What you tell the client
In the form consent line and on the call, say: what you collect, why, how long you keep it, and that they can ask for it to be deleted at any time.
